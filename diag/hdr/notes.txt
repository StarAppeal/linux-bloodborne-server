FINDING 1: the UI mask covers nearly the whole frame, so the gain is 1 nearly everywhere.
  hdr_gain.comp counts a pixel as UI where the display buffer differs from the hudless copy by
  more than 2-6/255 (gamma space). Here ui>0.5 on 99-100% of mid-tone and bright pixels (and
  90-95% of near-black ones), at every spot, with almost no UI on screen. So weight = 0 and the
  gain stays 1. With DLSS (Scaled presets) the hudless copy comes from CaptureScaledHudless
  (ui_image right after the upscale); the display buffer the gain pass compares it with differs
  from it nearly everywhere: wrong frame, wrong format/encoding (decode_srgb or a view of the copy
  in the display buffer's format), or a pass after the upscale (sharpen, the game's AA/grain or a
  gamma pass) that the copy misses. Not yet pinned down: the hudless copy itself is not dumped.
  Next step: dump hudless next to display/hdr_gain on BB_FRAME_DUMP_TRIGGER.

FINDING 2: the game's 16F scene holds very little above SDR white, so even a perfect mask gives
small gains.
