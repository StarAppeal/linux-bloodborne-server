BLOODBORNE SEAMLESS CO-OP (BLOODBORNE PC, THE NATIVE PORT)
==========================================================

Seamless co-op for Bloodborne, in the spirit of Elden Ring Seamless Co-op: up to four players
make a team, share one world, join each other without bells, signs or Insight, keep playing together through lamp
trips, deaths and boss fights, and progress is shared. The game runs natively on Windows (the
Bloodborne PC port), not in an emulator.

NO GAME FILES ARE INCLUDED. You need your own copy of Bloodborne for PlayStation 4, version
1.09: a decrypted dump of the game folder (the one with eboot.bin, sce_sys and dvdroot_ps4)
with the 1.09 update, or the PS4 .pkg files of the game and the update.

EVERYONE IN A PARTY USES THIS SAME RELEASE. Mixing releases, or mixing with other co-op kits or
shadPS4, is not supported.

YOU NEED
  - Windows 10 (1903 or later) or 11, 64-bit, and a Vulkan 1.3 graphics card with a current
    driver. Nothing else.
  - Bloodborne 1.09 (see above). Tested with CUSA03173 (the European Game of the Year edition);
    every player in a party should use the same edition.
  - A way for everyone to reach the PC that runs the co-op server (the host's): the same VPN
    (Radmin VPN, Tailscale, ZeroTier) or its public IP with ports forwarded: TCP 31313, 31315,
    20443 and UDP 31314. UDP 31314 must be open: every game's co-op traffic goes through the
    server on it.

STEPS (everyone)
  1. Run the setup .exe. Keep "Create a desktop shortcut" ticked if you want one.
  2. Open "Bloodborne Seamless Co-op" (the desktop shortcut or the Start menu). On the Co-op card:
       Your Bloodborne folder  the launcher looks for it by itself; else press Browse (it searches
                               inside and around the folder you pick) or Install from PKG
       You are                 one player is the Host (runs the co-op server on their PC),
                               the others are Players
       Host's IP address       the host's VPN or public IP (the host types their own)
       Account name/password   pick any; your account is made on the host's server the first
                               time you press PLAY
       Enemies                 the same for every player
  3. Press PLAY. The first time, Windows asks once to let co-op through its firewall (say yes),
     and the launcher prepares the game from your copy (links to it, nothing is copied) and makes
     the co-op map event files. The host's co-op server starts by itself with the host's PLAY.
  4. Continue, Load Game or New Game opens the team screen: create a team and invite the
     hunters online, or join a team you were invited to, then Ready. The leader's "Begin the
     hunt" starts everyone; the leader hosts the team's world. Load in and you are in one world
     with your team a few seconds later, no bells or signs. "Play alone" plays without a team.
     The first start takes longer (it builds its shader cache).

Co-op runs at 60 FPS. Picking Unlocked (up to 120 FPS) or 90 FPS as the frame rate is still
in testing: the launcher warns when you pick one. The game's updates still go out as often as at
60 (sent more often, they made the others freeze and fly).

SAVES
  Your saves live in the "user\savedata" folder of the install. "Bring saves from another
  folder" on the Co-op card copies a character from a shadPS4 'user' folder, an older co-op
  folder or a folder of CUSA... saves.

PARTY DEATH (AS IN ELDEN RING NIGHTREIGN)
  - A killing blow puts you down at 1 HP instead of killing you while a partner stands on the
    same map. Enemies leave you alone while you are down.
  - A partner revives you by striking you (R1 or R2 right next to you). Each strike counts their
    weapon's attack rating: they must deal your max HP the first time, twice that the next, four
    times after that (this starts over after 3 minutes without going down, or at a lamp).
  - Hold Circle for 3 seconds to give up: you die as before and wake at your lamp.
  - When everyone on a map is down, everyone there dies, drops their echoes and wakes at their
    own last lamp with the map reset.

CONTROLS AND SETTINGS
  - Insert (or L3+R3 on a gamepad) opens the port's menu in the game: resolution, upscaler (FSR,
    DLSS on RTX cards, XeSS), effects, controls. "Apply and restart" keeps co-op on.
  - The launcher's Advanced view has every other setting of the port.

UPDATING
  Run the new release's setup .exe: it installs over the old one. Your saves, settings and shader
  cache stay.

PROBLEMS
  - Black screen at start: delete user\shader in the install folder and start again.
  - Logs for a bug report: user\log\shad_log.txt and console.txt (and the .prev ones from the
    start before), the host's user\log\shadnet.txt and backend.txt.
  - Report issues at https://github.com/lKaniel/bloodborne-server/issues
