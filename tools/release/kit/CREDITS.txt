THE PROJECTS THIS IS FORKED FROM
  shadPS4                  https://github.com/shadps4-emu/shadPS4 (GPL-2.0-or-later)
                           The PS4 emulator. Its renderer and shader recompiler draw the game in
                           the port; its network and NP libraries carry the online play.
  shadp2p by Wozzardman    https://github.com/Wozzardman/shadp2p (GPL-2.0-or-later)
                           shadPS4 with peer-to-peer networking and Bloodborne online play. The
                           seamless co-op code started here (emulator\ in the repository).
  shadnet-p2p by Wozzardman  https://github.com/Wozzardman/shadnet-p2p (GPL-2.0-or-later)
                           The shadNet server (accounts, matchmaking, signaling) with P2P
                           support, by the shadPS4 team and Wozzardman. Our co-op server
                           (server\shadnet.exe) is this with the Bloodborne summon broker and the
                           party boards added.
  Bloodborne PC by deadinside28  https://github.com/deadinside28/bloodborne_pc (GPL-2.0-or-later)
                           The native port: the game's own code runs on your CPU, with shadPS4's
                           renderer. Releases 0.4, 0.5 and 0.51 are merged in.
  Windows port by Supermedo  https://github.com/Supermedo/bloodborne_pc (GPL-2.0-or-later)
                           The port on Windows, the launcher in 13 languages, DLSS, FSR 3.1/4.
  AdrianCsT (now 0xCydral)  https://github.com/0xCydral/bloodborne_pc (GPL-2.0-or-later)
                           Windows releases 1.6 and 1.7: the redesigned launcher, Install from
                           PKG, frame generation, XeSS, ReShade, the mouse camera and many fixes.
  The local backend        backend\bb_dummy_server.py started as the "LocalServer" stand-in for
                           the game's online services that came with the Bloodborne PC Online
                           community package; extended here for co-op.

  Inspired by Elden Ring Seamless Co-op (LukeYui) and the party death of Elden Ring Nightreign.
  No code from either.
